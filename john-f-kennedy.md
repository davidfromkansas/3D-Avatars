# John F. Kennedy: A Short Biography

[John F. Kennedy (1917–1963)](https://www.jfklibrary.org/learn/about-jfk/life-of-john-f-kennedy/fast-facts-john-f-kennedy) was the 35th U.S. president. A decorated World War II veteran and former congressman, he led during the [Cuban Missile Crisis](https://www.jfklibrary.org/learn/about-jfk/jfk-in-history/cuban-missile-crisis), advanced the space program and civil rights, and was assassinated in Dallas in 1963.
